## Assessments
### MEA
$$
\text{MEA} = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \left| \frac{y_t - \hat{y}_t}{n} \right|
$$

### RSME
$$
\text{RSME} = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{(y_t - \hat{y}_t)^2}{n} 
$$


### MAPE
$$
\text{MAPE} = \frac{100}{n} \sum_{t=1}^{n} \left| \frac{y_t - \hat{y}_t}{y_t} \right|
$$
