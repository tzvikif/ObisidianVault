# Expectation

## definition

$\mathbb{E}[X]$=$\displaystyle \sum_{x}x\cdot P(X=x)$.
## Expectation of function of $X$
let $X$ be a [[Random variable|r.v]]
$\mathbb{E}[g(X)]$ ?
we can define  a new [[Random variable|r.v]] $Y=g(X)$
$\mathbb{E}[Y]$=$\displaystyle \sum_{y}y\cdot P(Y=y)$.
a more useful approach is to stay with $X$:
$\mathbb{E}[g(X)]$=$\displaystyle \sum_{x}g(x)\cdot{}P(X=x)$
