# epistemic uncertainty

Epistemic uncertainty comes from lack of knowledge.

Epistemic uncertainty=model uncertainty+approximation uncertainty

## model uncertainty
uncertainty about whether the chosen hypothesis space ${H}$ is appropriate

## Approximation uncertainty
uncertainty because $\hat{h}$ was learned from finite data and may differ from $h∗$

This uncertainty can, in principle, be reduced by:

- more training data,
- better features,
- better model class,
- better inductive bias,
- better coverage of sparse regions.
