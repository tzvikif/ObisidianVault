# aleatoric uncertainty

Aleatoric uncertainty comes from the true conditional distribution $p(y|x)$
Even if the learner knew the true distribution perfectly, the outcome might still be uncertain.
**example 1**
$p(y=1|x)=0.6$
$p(y=0|x)=0.4$
Even the best possible model cannot know the exact label with certainty. The uncertainty is part of the data-generating process.
**example 2**
Suppose the model sees a very low-resolution image.
$p(cat|x)=0.5$
$p(dog|x)=0.5$
