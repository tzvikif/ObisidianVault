# p-value

The *p-value* converts the [[t-statistic]] into a probability.

It answers:
“If the [[null hypothesis]] were true, how likely would we observe a [[t-statistic]] at least this extreme?”
### formally:
$$
p=P(|T|\ge|t_{observed}|\;|H_{0})
$$
